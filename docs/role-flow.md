# Role Flow

## Join

1. Member masuk server.
2. Bot beri role `UNVERIFIED`.
3. Member hanya lihat channel awal hasil setup template.

## Verify

1. Member klik tombol verify.
2. Bot hapus role `UNVERIFIED`.
3. Bot tambah role `MEMBER`.

## Self Role

Self role menggunakan select menu:

- `ENHANCED`
- `LEGACY`
- `PROMO PING`
- `EVENT PING`
